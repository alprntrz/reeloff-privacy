ReelOff Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

ReelOff is an iPhone app that blocks the apps you choose and lets you use Instagram without Reels. This policy explains what happens to your data when you use it.

Summary

  • ReelOff has no accounts and no servers of its own.
  • We do not collect, store or sell your personal data.
  • Everything ReelOff saves stays on your iPhone.

What ReelOff stores on your iPhone

  • Your blocked apps. The apps and categories you choose to block are saved as tokens provided by Apple's Screen Time. The tokens do not tell ReelOff which apps you have installed or how you use them.
  • App settings. Whether you have finished setup.
  • Instagram website data. When you sign in to Instagram inside ReelOff, the Instagram website saves cookies and similar data in the app, the same way it would in a web browser. This keeps you signed in.

None of this is sent to us.

Instagram

ReelOff shows the Instagram website (instagram.com) inside the app. You connect directly to Instagram. Your username, password, messages and activity go to Instagram, not to us. Instagram handles that data under its own privacy policy: https://privacycenter.instagram.com/policy

ReelOff adds a small script to the page that hides Reels and Explore. The script does not read, store or send your password, messages, posts or any other content.

Screen Time

ReelOff uses Apple's Screen Time (Family Controls) to block the apps you select. You grant this access yourself and can remove it at any time in Settings > Screen Time. ReelOff does not read or export your Screen Time activity.

Purchases

Subscriptions are sold and billed by Apple. We never see your payment details. ReelOff checks on your device, through Apple, whether your subscription is active. Apple gives us aggregated sales and usage reports that do not identify you.

Analytics, advertising and tracking

ReelOff contains no analytics, advertising or tracking software. It does not track you across apps or websites.

Children

ReelOff is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Deleting your data

Delete ReelOff from your iPhone to remove everything it stored, including the Instagram sign-in saved in the app. To delete data held by Instagram, use Instagram's own tools.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
